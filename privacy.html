Privacy Policy

Helmets - 2 for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 2 November 2026

In short: the app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your schedule, markings, notes and salary stay on your device. The only things that reach the internet are the holiday list download and, unless you remove them, Google's ads.

What stays on your device

Your company, shift and start time, your day markings and notes, your holiday changes, saved calculations and (only if you choose “Remember on this iPhone”) your salary are stored on your device. They are shared with the app's own widgets on the same device, and are not sent to us or anyone else. Shift reminders are scheduled on your device.

Backups

If you use “Save a backup”, the app writes a file to the place you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. That file is under your control and is subject to the policy of the storage you pick. The app never uploads it anywhere.

Holiday updates

The app downloads a small list of Kuwait's official holidays from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages). The request contains no personal information. Like any website, GitHub may log your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Ads

The app shows a banner from Google AdMob on the Calculators and Settings screens. Ads are requested as non-personalised: the app does not ask to track you and does not give Google your advertising identifier. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google still receives information such as your IP address, device type and system version, and how you interact with the ad. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying “Remove ads” turns ads off completely. The purchase is handled by Apple; we receive no payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: Help and feedback.

Helmets - 2 is an independent app. It is not made by, or affiliated with, the developers of the original HELMETS app, KNPC, KOC or PIC.
